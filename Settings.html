Install app

Browser

Add BASE to your home screen for a full-screen, app-like experience that works offline. Your data lives on your device and stays put across app updates — updating the app never wipes what you've entered.

On iPhone / iPad: tap the Share icon in Safari, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, open the browser menu and tap Install app / Add to Home screen.

    Sync between devices

    Off

    Keep BASE the same on your Android app and your iPhone. Your data is encrypted on this phone with your passphrase and stored in a private gist in your GitHub account, so GitHub can't read it. Use the same token and passphrase on each device.

    1. Create a GitHub token with only the gist permission. Set the expiration to No expiration, or remember to renew it. Copy it.
    2. Paste it below and choose a sync passphrase. You'll need the same passphrase on your other device, and it can't be recovered.

    Rojo on Android

    Android app only

    Rojo lives in BASE (tap him in the top bar). In the Android app he can also float over your other apps: tap him to ask about what you're looking at, update balances or add bills. During a focus session he notices if you open a distracting app (social, video, games) and gives you a nudge. Drag him to move him; long-press to hide him until you next unlock. Your screen is only read when you tap him, and it's sent to Claude with your question using the key above.

    Rojo floating over other apps needs the BASE Android app. Everywhere else, tap Rojo in the top bar.
    1. Claude API keySave it in the Claude AI section above.
    2. Allow restricted settingsAndroid blocks step 3 for apps installed outside the Play Store. Open App info, tap ⋮ (top right) → Allow restricted settings, and confirm. (If you don't see ⋮, skip this.)
    3. Turn on Rojo (BASE)Accessibility → Installed apps → Rojo (BASE) → On. This lets him float, read your screen when you tap him, and nudge you during focus.
    4. NotificationsFor bill reminders. Then turn on reminders on the Budget page, or just ask the agent.
