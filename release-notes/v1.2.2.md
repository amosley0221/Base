## BASE v1.2.2 — a fresh start

- **No more sample data.** Earlier versions started with example bills, cards, tasks, notes, classes and a name, and saved them as if they were yours. That's where the "8 bills overdue" came from. Updating removes those examples automatically. Anything you added yourself stays, and so do your Scoreboard teams.
- **First-time welcome.** Home asks what to call you, then gives you shortcuts to set up your budget and bills, tasks, week, school and the floating agent. Skip it anytime.
- **New bills don't start out overdue.** A bill you add mid-month (say rent due the 1st, added on the 18th) starts with its *next* due date instead of showing as overdue for this month.
- The agent no longer says you're "short" for bills until you've entered at least one account balance.
- Settings → Reset → *Restore sample data* still brings the examples back if you ever want to look around.
