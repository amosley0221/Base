## BASE v1.4.0 — meet Rojo

**Rojo is your BASE assistant:** a red fuzzball with round glasses. Tap him in the top bar of any page to open his screen.

- **He's alive.** His eyes follow your finger, he blinks, hops, tilts his head while listening, gets wide-eyed when he's happy, and dozes off (Zzz) if you leave him alone. Tap him to say hi.
- **Hold him to talk.** Press and hold Rojo (or the mic button) and speak, then let go. He answers in a speech bubble with little chirps instead of a voice. You can also type. The speaker button mutes the chirps.
- **Same powers as before:** ask anything, update balances, add bills, set reminders, mark things paid. Undo is right in the bubble. The chat-bubble button shows the whole conversation.
- **Focus timer.** Say "I'm doing calc homework for 45 minutes" and Rojo starts a session: the task at the top, a big countdown, and pause / +5 / end buttons. He cheers at halfway and with five minutes left, celebrates when you finish, and the time left shows under him in the top bar.
- **Off-task nudges (Android).** During a focus session, if you sit in a distracting app (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, games…) for about 20 seconds, the floating Rojo pops up with a nudge, a little buzz and a chirp. You also get a "time's up" notification even if BASE is closed.
- **The floating bubble is now mini-Rojo**, and the ↗ button in his chat window opens his full screen.

**Setup:** the first time you hold Rojo to talk, Android asks for microphone access. Off-task nudges need the same accessibility switch as before; it's now called **Rojo (BASE)** in Settings → Accessibility.

Installs over v1.3.2 (same key, in place; your data stays).
