## BASE v1.1.4 — horizontal scroll / right gap, fixed at the source

This time the fix was **measured, not guessed**. I rendered the app in a mobile browser at phone and unfolded widths and listed every element wider than the screen. On phones, Android's browser engine **widens the whole page** to fit anything too wide, which is why the earlier "clip it" fixes never worked. The overflow itself had to go:

- **Habits** — two side-by-side cards couldn't shrink below ~298px each, so on the unfolded Fold they pushed the page wider than the screen. They now sit two per row only when there's room, otherwise one per row.
- **Your Week** — the day columns and the code-style class block refused to shrink; they now fit their space.
- **Budget** — the cash-flow month switcher and the Cards & accounts panel no longer push past narrow screens.

Verified: no horizontal overflow on any page from 360px phones through the full unfolded range.
