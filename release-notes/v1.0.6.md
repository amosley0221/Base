## BASE v1.0.6 — remove the right-side rail on wide screens

The "bar" down the right edge of the unfolded / tablet view was the vertical **section-dot navigation rail** (the column of dots with the HOME label). It's a scroll aid that the wide scrolling layout doesn't need, so it's now hidden on wide screens — the edge is clean, and the hero's right-hand info uses the full width. Phones keep the rail.

Installs over v1.0.5 with no uninstall; also reaches the installed app from the website on reopen.
