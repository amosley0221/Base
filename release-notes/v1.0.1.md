## BASE v1.0.1 — budgeting, reminders & a Fold-friendly layout

### Budget / bill tracking
- **Bill reminders & notifications** — get a heads-up before bills are due. Turn it on in **Budget → Bill reminders** and pick how many days ahead. Opening the app always catches you up; where your phone supports it, reminders also fire in the background.
- **Recurring auto-advance** — bills, subscriptions and card payments roll forward to their next cycle automatically. Tap **✓** to mark one paid and it advances; your payment history is kept.
- **Monthly cash flow** — a new month-by-month view: income in, bills out (paid vs left), net, and your **projected end-of-month cash** after remaining bills. Step through past/future months.
- **Overdue flagging** — overdue bills are called out on the Home summary and in Budget.
- Yearly subscriptions can now specify which month they hit.

### Theme
- The **status bar at the top now matches your theme** — near-black in dark/matcha mode, white in light mode (no more mismatched red bar). The app launch splash is dark to match.

### Layout
- **Unfolded / foldable & tablet optimization** — the Home hero no longer collides with the section rail on the Galaxy Z Fold's inner screen, and wider screens get a calmer, better-proportioned layout.

Installs over v1.0.0 with no uninstall. Most of these also reach the installed app automatically once the website updates — this APK bakes in the native launch/splash changes.
