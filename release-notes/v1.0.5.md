## BASE v1.0.5 — fix the right-edge bar (unfolded)

Fixes the dark vertical bar that appeared down the right side of the screen on the unfolded Fold / tablet view. It came from `viewport-fit=cover` extending the page under the display cutout, leaving an unpainted strip on the right. Removed it — the layout now fills edge to edge, and the status bar still matches your theme.

Installs over v1.0.4 with no uninstall; also reaches the installed app from the website on reopen.
