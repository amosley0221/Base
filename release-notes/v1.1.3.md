## BASE v1.1.3 — stop the horizontal scroll / right gap

The right-edge gap came from a stray horizontal scroll. My earlier fix used `overflow-x: clip`, which the Android System WebView on your device silently ignores — so it never took. Switched to the universally-supported `overflow-x: hidden` on the page root (plus blocking horizontal overscroll), so there's no sideways scroll and no gap, folded or unfolded.

Installs over v1.1.2 (same key, in-place — your data stays).
