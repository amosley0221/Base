## BASE v1.8.1 — floating Rojo fixes

- Fixed: dragging the floating Rojo to the other side of the screen (like the left edge) could crash BASE. He now slides over to that side.
- Fixed: after you opened the chat from the floating Rojo and closed it, Rojo sometimes disappeared until you locked and unlocked your phone. He now comes right back when the chat closes.

Installs over v1.8.0 (same key, in place; your data stays).
