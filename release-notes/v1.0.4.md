## BASE v1.0.4 — wide layout, done right

The unfolded view now keeps the **same cinematic scroll and animations as the phone** — it no longer swaps to a separate dashboard. Instead, on the wide screen each section uses the space as **tile on the left, details on the right**:

- **Hero** — greeting on the left; weather *and* an at-a-glance stack (classes today, next bill, top task) on the right.
- **Every section** (Focus, Habits, School, Budget, Scoreboard, Week) — the heading/summary sits on the left while its detail fills the right.

Phones (folded) are unchanged — single column, full cinematic scroll.

Also under the hood: app updates now apply in a single reopen (no more new-layout-with-old-styles), so future versions land cleanly.

Installs over v1.0.3 with no uninstall; also reaches the installed app from the website on reopen.
