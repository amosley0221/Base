## BASE v1.6.0 — Rojo on your computer

- **Rojo Desktop for Windows and Mac** is out (see the "Rojo Desktop" release). Once it's installed and linked, tell Rojo on your phone *"on my PC, …"* and the task runs on your computer. When it's done, Rojo tells you the result here.
- **Windows / Mac switch.** In **Settings → Rojo on your computer**, choose which computer "my computer" means. The panel also shows when each computer was last online.
- Both need **Sync between devices** turned on with the same token and passphrase.

Installs over v1.5.0 (same key, in place; your data stays).
