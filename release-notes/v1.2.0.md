## BASE v1.2.0 — the floating BASE agent

**Meet your BASE buddy.** A little blob that floats over your other apps: **green in dark mode, red in light mode**. Tap it and a chat sheet slides up over whatever you're doing.

- **Ask anything.** It's Claude, using your own API key (the same one as the essay assistant).
- **Ask about what's on your screen.** Looking at a site or an app? Ask "what does this say?" or "is this a good deal?" and it reads the page and answers. It only reads the screen when you tap it.
- **Keeps your budget up to date.** Say "Checking has $1,240" and the balance updates in BASE. Say "Rent is $1,200 due on the 1st" and it saves the bill and turns on reminders. It can also tell you what's due and whether you're short this month, and mark bills as paid.
- **Undo** under every change it makes. If it has read your screen or the web in a chat, any change waits for you to tap **Apply**, so a website can't make it change your budget.
- **Nudges.** When something is overdue, due soon, or you're short for this month's bills, a little bubble pops up next to the blob when you unlock your phone. Tap it to talk about it, or tap ✕ to dismiss.
- Drag the blob to move it (it snaps to the edge). Long-press it to hide it until your next unlock.

**Bill reminders now really work in the app.** Before this update, Android didn't deliver them. They're now proper Android notifications: one heads-up a few days before each bill and one on the day, even when BASE is closed.

### Setup (one time)
Open **Settings → Floating agent** and follow the checklist:
1. Save your Claude API key (Settings → Claude AI).
2. **Allow restricted settings.** Tap **App info**, then **⋮** (top right) → **Allow restricted settings**. Android requires this for apps installed outside the Play Store before it will let you do step 3.
3. **Turn on BASE Agent.** Tap **Accessibility** → **Installed apps** → **BASE Agent** → On. Android will warn that it can see your screen; that's what lets the agent read the page when you tap it.
4. Allow notifications for bill reminders.

Installs over v1.1.5 (same key, in place; your data stays). If anything looks off, v1.1.5 is still on the Releases page to reinstall.
