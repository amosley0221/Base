## BASE v1.5.0 — Rojo uses your phone, speaks Spanish, and remembers

**Rojo can use your phone (Android).** Ask him to do something in another app or site, like *"post 'Building something cool' on X"*.
- He opens the app or page, finds the right field and button, types and taps, step by step.
- While he works, the screen edges **glow red** and his pointer flies to each spot before he taps it.
- A pill at the top shows what he's doing, with a **Stop** button.
- **You approve anything final:** taps that post, send, buy, pay or delete wait for **Allow**.
- He never types passwords or card numbers. If a site needs you to log in, he stops and asks you to do that part.
- When he's done, his bubble tells you what he did.

**His eyes follow you.** The floating Rojo glances toward whatever you tap in other apps.

**The screen glows while he listens.** When you hold Rojo to talk, the screen edges light up. They glow more softly while he's thinking.

**Spanish practice.** Tap **ES** on Rojo's screen, or say "let's practice Spanish".
- He uses simple Spanish with English help, and gently corrects your sentences (✏️).
- He switches to English when you're lost.
- Hold-to-talk listens for Spanish while practice is on.

**Rojo remembers.** Tell him "remember…" and he keeps it between chats on all your devices. See or edit what he knows in **Settings → Rojo's memory**. **Copy for ChatGPT** and **Add from ChatGPT** move memories between Rojo and your ChatGPT Dot.

**Sideways full screen.** Turn your phone sideways on Rojo's screen and he fills it, peeking up from the bottom like Bluey.

**One-time step for phone control:** Android may need you to turn **Rojo (BASE)** off and back on in Settings → Accessibility so it picks up the new "perform gestures" permission.

Installs over v1.4.0 (same key, in place; your data stays).
