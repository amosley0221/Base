## BASE v1.3.1 — fix for older saved data

- **Fixed: BASE could get stuck on a blank Home** ("Today", "Looking at your day…") when your saved data came from an older version. Filling in missing settings tried to read them from the old data instead of the built-in defaults and crashed. This is what broke the iPhone version.
- **On-screen error report.** If something ever fails to load, BASE now shows what broke, with a **Fix & reload** button that clears the stored copy of the app's files (never your data).

Installs over v1.3.0 (same key, in place; your data stays).
