## BASE v1.8.0 — Rojo takes over the screen, and offers help in your apps

- **Full-screen Rojo while linked to your computer.** When Rojo's screen is open and your computer is connected (green dot on the computer icon), he fills the screen and peeks up from the bottom like Bluey, whether the phone is upright, sideways or unfolded. His eyes follow your computer's mouse.
- **Rojo offers help in other apps.** Open your fantasy football app during the season and Rojo pops up: *"It's football Sunday! 🏈 Want lineup suggestions before kickoff?"* Tap **Yes** and he reads your lineup, checks injuries, byes and news, and suggests who to start or sit. He'll ask if you want him to make the swaps. Tap **✕** for "not today". He asks at most once a day, and on Tuesdays and Wednesdays he offers waiver pickups instead. Until you tap Yes he only knows which app is open, not what's on the screen.
- Turn offers off in **Settings → Rojo on Android → Let Rojo offer help in other apps**.
- The floating Rojo now steps aside while you're inside BASE (BASE has its own Rojo up top), so there aren't two of him on Rojo's screen.

Installs over v1.7.1 (same key, in place; your data stays).
