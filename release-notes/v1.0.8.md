## BASE v1.0.8 — unfolded navigation, no right gap, EPL teams

- **Edit teams (and reach every section) when unfolded.** Hiding the dot-rail removed the way to open a section's full page on wide screens. Each section tile now has an **"Open →"** link — so you can jump to Scoreboard to edit teams, Plan, Budget, etc.
- **No more right-edge gap / horizontal scroll when unfolded.** Stray horizontal overflow is now clipped on wide screens (without breaking the scroll animations).
- **EPL / soccer teams.** The add-team picker now tries the teams endpoint both with and without the page-limit param and still falls back to standings, so soccer leagues like EPL list their clubs. (If a league ever still won't load its teams, use **Follow leagues** to get its live scores without picking a specific team.)

Installs over v1.0.7 with no uninstall; reaches the installed app from the website on reopen.
