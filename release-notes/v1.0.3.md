## BASE v1.0.3 — a real unfolded dashboard

Unfolding your phone (or opening BASE on a tablet) now switches to a **dedicated dashboard layout** instead of the stretched phone view. Everything at a glance, tiled on one screen with minimal scrolling:

- **Today** — your focus tasks, check them off right here
- **Budget** — cash, due this month, next bill (overdue flagged)
- **School** — current GPA and your next assignments
- **Upcoming** — today's schedule and countdowns
- **Habits** — tap this week's dots inline
- **Scoreboard** — today's games for your teams

Your phone (folded) keeps the full cinematic scrolling experience — the dashboard only appears on wide screens. Anything you change on the dashboard stays in sync with the rest of the app.

Installs over v1.0.2 with no uninstall. Also reaches the installed app automatically once the website updates — reopen BASE to see it.
