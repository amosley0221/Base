## BASE v1.7.0 — Rojo on your phone drives your computer

- **Your phone is Rojo, your computer is his hands.** With Rojo Desktop 1.1 on your Windows PC or Mac (linked through Sync), open Rojo here while you're on the same Wi-Fi: his **eyes follow your computer's mouse**, you talk to him on the phone, and he clicks and types on the computer.
- A **computer icon** at the top of Rojo's screen shows when it's connected (green dot). Tap it for details.
- **Allow / Don't** for anything final (posting, sending, buying, deleting) shows right in Rojo's bubble, and on the computer too. First answer wins.
- When the computer isn't reachable, *"on my PC, …"* tasks queue like before and run when it's on.
- Rojo's eyes no longer slide over his glasses when he looks into a corner.

Installs over v1.6.0 (same key, in place; your data stays).
