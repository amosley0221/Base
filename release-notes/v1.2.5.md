## BASE v1.2.5 — live scores on Home, and your weather location

- **Live scores for your teams.** ESPN's per-team schedule doesn't include live scores, which is why the Eagles card showed "null–null". BASE now fills them in from the league scoreboard. Home says things like *"Your Eagles are down 14–19 vs Panthers · 10:37 - 4th"*, and after the game *"won 24–17"* or *"lost"*. Tap the team name to jump to the Scoreboard.
- **Weather uses your real location.** The app never had permission to use your location, so it always fell back to New York. The first time Home loads after updating, Android will ask; "approximate" is enough.
- **Pick a city instead:** Settings → **Weather location**, search for your city, tap it. Or tap the location name under the weather on Home. "Use my current location" switches back.

Installs over v1.2.4 (same key, in place; your data stays).
