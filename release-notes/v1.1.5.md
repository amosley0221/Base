## BASE v1.1.5 — section dots on the unfolded screen

- **Section-dot rail is back when unfolded**, the same as folded. Tap or drag the dots to jump between sections.
- **The grey scrollbar is gone.** That bar was Android's own built-in scroll indicator (page styling can't hide it), so it's now switched off in the app. The dots take its place.
- **No overlap on wide screens.** The active section label (HOME, HABITS, …) shows while you scroll and fades about a second after you stop, so it doesn't sit on top of your cards. Folded behaves exactly as before.

Checked before release: no page is wider than the screen at any phone or unfolded width, and the unfolded and folded views were reviewed in screenshots.

Installs over v1.1.4 (same key, in place; your data stays). If anything looks off, v1.1.4 is still on the Releases page to reinstall.
