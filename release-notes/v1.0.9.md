## BASE v1.0.9 — hide the status bar when unfolded

When unfolded (wide screen), BASE now goes fullscreen so there's no mismatched grey system bar at the top. Because browsers only allow fullscreen after a tap, it engages on your **first tap or scroll** after unfolding, and the status bar comes back when you fold to portrait (where it already matches). Phones are unchanged.

Installs over v1.0.8 with no uninstall; reaches the installed app from the website on reopen.
